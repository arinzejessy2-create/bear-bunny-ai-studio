Growth facts
What affects final height?

Genetics is a major factor. Growth also depends on puberty timing, health, nutrition and whether growth plates remain open. Exercise, stretching and hanging do not lengthen the leg or spine bones.

What to do if you're concerned

Ask a healthcare professional to review your growth chart and development. A bone-age X-ray may help estimate skeletal maturity. Growth hormone is only for specific diagnosed conditions and must be prescribed and monitored by a specialist.

Safety

Avoid height pills, unprescribed hormones, extreme stretching and unsafe inversion devices. Keep exercise comfortable and pain-free.
